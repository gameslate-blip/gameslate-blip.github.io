Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the StringSlate Android app handles. StringSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database (Raphael Lira).

1. No account required

StringSlate has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data.

2. Microphone

The tuner and the intonation helper use the microphone only while they are on screen, and only after you allow the microphone permission. The sound is analysed on your phone to find its pitch. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the developer never receives it. If you deny the permission, the tuner and the intonation helper do not work, but the setup guides and the guitar checklist still do.

3. Information on your device

The guitars you add, their checklists, your tuning choice and your language are stored only on your phone. You can remove them by deleting a guitar, clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version of StringSlate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) and manages consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0).

Each time the app starts, it updates consent information through UMP and shows a consent message when one is required for your region. StringSlate requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When privacy options are required, the app shows an "Ad privacy choices" button in the About tab where you can review or change your choices.

According to Google's data disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google processes this information under Google's Privacy Policy. The ads SDK does not receive the microphone audio.

StringSlate does not run its own analytics.

5. Security

All network requests made by the app (for ads) use HTTPS; unencrypted connections are blocked.

6. Children

StringSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. StringSlate keeps no personal data on any server of its own. For advertising data, use the "Ad privacy choices" button or Google's tools. You can also contact us with any request.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: gameslate@proton.me.
