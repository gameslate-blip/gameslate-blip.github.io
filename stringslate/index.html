Guitar tuner and setup guides

Tune it.
Set it up. Play.

A tuner and step-by-step setup guides for acoustic and electric guitar. Available in English and Portuguese, in portrait and landscape.

What's inside
1 · Tune

A tuner that listens

Nine tunings, an adjustable A4 reference, automatic string detection and a note you can play to tune by ear.

2 · Set up

Illustrated guides

Strings, neck relief, string action, nut, intonation and pickup height, with typical values, tools and safety warnings.

3 · Check

Intonation helper

Play the open string, then the 12th fret. StringSlate tells you how many cents off you are and which way to move the saddle.

StringSlate does not require an account. The microphone is used only while the tuner is on screen; the sound is analysed on your phone and is never recorded or sent anywhere. Setup values are typical references; follow your guitar maker's specifications and take the guitar to a technician if you are unsure.
