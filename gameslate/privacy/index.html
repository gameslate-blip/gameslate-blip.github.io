Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the GameSlate Android app handles. GameSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database.

1. No account required

GameSlate does not offer account creation, sign-up, or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data. There is no GameSlate account to delete.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Favorites: games you save (Steam app ID, name, and image address) are stored in private app storage on your device. They remain until you remove them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Android device backup or device-to-device transfer may include this favorites file.
  • Cache: to load faster and use less data, public game data the app downloads (such as prices, reviews, player counts, and ProtonDB ratings) is kept in the app's cache and reused for up to an hour. The cache is limited in size, stays on your device, is not included in backups, and can be cleared in Android's app settings.
  • Search text is not saved by the app, including in the cache.

3. Information sent to game-data providers

To show game information, the app requests it from these services over HTTPS:

  • Steam Store (store.steampowered.com): search, game details, Linux support, prices, featured lists, categories, and similar games.
  • Steam Web API (api.steampowered.com): current player counts and review summaries.
  • SteamData.dev (api.steamdata.dev): popularity feeds, trends, recent releases, and player history.
  • ProtonDB (www.protondb.com): Linux compatibility summaries and community reports.
  • Steam image servers (cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com and other steamstatic.com hosts): game artwork.

When you search, your search text is sent to the Steam Store. Requests to the Steam Store also include the two-letter country code from your device's language and region settings (for example, "br"), so results, prices, and availability match your region. GameSlate does not use your location. Other requests contain only public game IDs or list requests. Like any website, these services can see your IP address. SteamDB, SteamData.dev, and ProtonDB pages open in your browser only when you tap a link to them. These providers process information under their own privacy terms.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version of GameSlate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) and manages consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0).

Each time the app starts, it updates consent information through UMP and shows a consent message when one is required for your region. GameSlate requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When UMP indicates that privacy options are required, the app shows an "Ad privacy choices" link where you can review or change your choices.

According to Google's data disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. IP address may be used to estimate general location. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google processes this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

GameSlate does not run its own analytics.

GameSlate offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove ads". Google Play handles the payment: the developer never receives your payment details, and the app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Google account. If you own it, ads are turned off and the ads SDK is not started.

5. Data retention and security

Favorites stay on your device until you remove them. Cached game data is reused for up to an hour and replaced over time. All network requests made by the app use HTTPS; unencrypted connections are blocked. Information received by Steam, SteamData.dev, ProtonDB, and Google is kept according to their own retention practices.

6. Children

GameSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because GameSlate keeps your data only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For advertising data, use the "Ad privacy choices" link in the app or Google's tools. You can also contact us with any request.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app's features or providers change; the date above shows the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact gameslate@proton.me.
