Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the BookSlate Android app handles. BookSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database (Raphael Lira).

1. No account required

BookSlate has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version of BookSlate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) and manages consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0).

Each time the app starts, it updates consent information through UMP and shows a consent message when one is required for your region. BookSlate requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When privacy options are required, the app shows an "Ad privacy choices" button in the About tab where you can review or change your choices.

According to Google's data disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google processes this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

BookSlate does not run its own analytics.

3. Information sent to Open Library

To find books and suggestions, the app sends requests over HTTPS to Open Library (openlibrary.org), including the text you type when searching and public book or subject identifiers. Book covers are loaded from covers.openlibrary.org. Like any website, Open Library can see your IP address and handles it under its own privacy policy. BookSlate does not send your list of books to the developer, and does not use your location, contacts or other sensitive personal data.

4. Information on your device

The language you choose is remembered on your device. The books you add are used only to produce suggestions and are not sent to the developer.

5. Children

BookSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. BookSlate keeps no personal data on any server of its own. For advertising data, use the "Ad privacy choices" button or Google's tools. You can also contact us with any request.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: gameslate@proton.me.
