Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the TrackSlate Android app handles. TrackSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database.

1. No account required

TrackSlate has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version of TrackSlate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) and manages consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0).

Each time the app starts, it updates consent information through UMP and shows a consent message when one is required for your region. TrackSlate requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When privacy options are required, the app shows an "Ad privacy choices" button in About where you can review or change your choices.

According to Google's data disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google processes this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

TrackSlate does not run its own analytics.

TrackSlate offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove ads". Google Play handles the payment: the developer never receives your payment details, and the app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Google account. If you own it, ads are turned off and the ads SDK is not started.

3. Your songs

The audio files you choose (with Android's file picker, or by sharing them to TrackSlate from another app) are read by the app and separated on your phone by a neural network that is part of the app. They are never uploaded, and the developer never receives them. The network runs with Microsoft's ONNX Runtime library; TrackSlate switches off that library's own usage reporting, so it sends nothing either.

TrackSlate has no song search and sends no information about your songs anywhere. The internet is used only by the advertising described above.

4. Information on your device

The four tracks made from a song, its title and length are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, until you delete the song, clear the app data or uninstall the app. They are not included in Android's backup. A track is copied elsewhere only when you use Save and choose the place yourself in Android's file picker. Your settings (the quality and whether you have seen the notice about music rights) are stored on your phone. TrackSlate does not use your microphone, camera, location or contacts. On Android 13 and later it asks for notification permission only to show the progress of a separation, which runs in the background and can take minutes, and the Pause button while music plays.

5. Children

TrackSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. TrackSlate keeps no personal data on any server of its own. For advertising data, use the "Ad privacy choices" button or Google's tools. You can also contact us with any request.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: gameslate@proton.me.
