Find a song's key and chords, then play along

What key is
that song in?

Let JamSlate listen to a song and it shows its key, tempo and chord progression, measured on your phone. Then a live band (drums, bass, piano and organ) plays along with you, even with the song's own chords. Available in English and Portuguese.

What's inside
1 · Listen

Phone audio, microphone or a file

Play the song in another app and JamSlate captures what the phone plays (Android 10 and newer), hold the phone near a speaker, or open an audio file. The sound is analysed on your phone and never saved or uploaded.

2 · Find

Key, tempo and chords

The key (with the next most likely keys one tap away), the tempo and the chord progression, for example A | E | F#m | D = I V vi IV. Listening is a beta feature: it can miss the key or chords, especially with real recordings, but it can still give you an idea to play from. Check it by ear and correct what you need.

3 · Play along

A band in the same key

One tap and the band plays the song's own chords, or pick a style: twelve-bar blues, pop, rock, waltz, jazz and modes. Change the tempo while it plays, tap it to match a song, and mute or mix each instrument.

JamSlate does not require an account, and the band plays fully offline. The microphone is used only while you press Listen, and Phone audio only after you agree in Android's capture screen (Android words it as “share your screen”: JamSlate takes only the sound, never the screen). The optional search by name uses MusicBrainz and AcousticBrainz; its tempos and keys are estimates. Sounds: MuseScore_General (MIT) played by FluidSynth (LGPL-2.1). JamSlate is independent and not affiliated with either of them or with any artist; it does not play any recording.
