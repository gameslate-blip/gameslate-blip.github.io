Backing band for practice and jamming

A live band
in your pocket.

Drums, bass, piano and organ that play with a live feel, in the style, key and tempo you pick, so you can practise and play along. Available in English and Portuguese, in portrait and landscape.

What's inside
1 · Pick

Style, key and tempo

Twelve-bar blues, pop, rock, waltz, jazz and modes, major or minor, in any key. Change the tempo while it plays, or mute and mix each instrument.

2 · Play along

Chords, scale and shapes

See the chord chart follow the music with a visual metronome, the home scale on the guitar neck, and how to play each chord.

3 · Search

Something similar to a song

Type a song and JamSlate suggests a style, key and tempo like it, ready to play. It does not play the song: it gives you a band to play along with.

JamSlate does not require an account, and the band plays fully offline. The optional song search uses MusicBrainz and AcousticBrainz; its tempos and keys are estimates. Sounds: MuseScore_General (MIT) played by FluidSynth (LGPL-2.1). JamSlate is independent and not affiliated with either of them or with any artist.
