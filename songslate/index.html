A songwriter's notebook with a band

Write it. Play it.
Record it.

Type your chords and words, press Play, and drums, bass and piano play your song with a live feel. Then record your voice or your guitar over it and share the result. Available in English and Portuguese.

What's inside
1 · Write

Chords above the words

Intro, verse, chorus, bridge and more. Every chord line is checked as you type, and you can transpose the whole song a semitone at a time.

2 · Play

A band that follows your song

Louder in the chorus, gentler in the verse. See the current chord, a visual metronome and the line you are on; tap a line to jump there or loop a section.

3 · Record

Takes, mix and share

Record as many takes as you like over the band, line them up, mix band, voice and reverb, and send the song as an audio file or the chord sheet as a PDF.

SongSlate does not require an account. Your songs and recordings stay on your phone; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a file. The microphone is used only while you record. Sounds: MuseScore_General (MIT) played by FluidSynth (LGPL-2.1); source and licences. SongSlate is independent.
